COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT Campaign activity

Building Bridges Through Community Engagement

Meaningful community engagement begins with listening, mutual respect and a willingness to recognise the contributions of different groups within society.

Promoting Inclusion and Participation

Across AMAC/Bwari Federal Constituency, communities are made up of people from different backgrounds, cultures and experiences. Their contributions, perspectives and aspirations form an important part of the constituency's social and economic life.

Comrade Collins Chimaobi Opara recognises the importance of engaging different community groups and encouraging constructive conversations around shared priorities and development.

Strengthening Relationships with Young People

Young people remain important participants in community development. Their ideas, energy, skills and perspectives can contribute meaningfully to building stronger and more responsive communities.

Engagement with youth groups provides an opportunity to encourage participation, understand their concerns and recognise the role they can play in shaping the future of the constituency.

A Shared Vision for Community Development

The campaign's approach to community engagement places emphasis on inclusion, dialogue, cooperation and shared responsibility.

The objective is to encourage an environment where residents and community stakeholders can contribute their ideas, identify common challenges and participate in conversations about sustainable development.

Moving Forward Together

Building stronger communities requires continuous engagement and cooperation. The campaign remains committed to promoting conversations that recognise the importance of every community and encourage participation in the development process.

Together, we can work towards a more inclusive, connected and prosperous AMAC/Bwari Federal Constituency.

A stronger constituency is built when communities are heard, respected and given the opportunity to participate in shaping their shared future.
