Labour Party National Leaders Visit Owerri to Flag Off Comrade Collins Chimaobi Opara's 2027 Campaign

11 November 2026 Owerri, Imo State Campaign Report

The Labour Party's National Working Committee and the party's National Leader, His Excellency Governor Alex Chioma Otti of Abia State, were in Owerri, Imo State, on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, for the flag-off of the 2027 National Assembly campaign of Comrade Collins Chimaobi Opara.

The gathering brought together party officials, leaders and supporters for the campaign event. The presence of Governor Otti and other Labour Party representatives formed part of the national party leadership's engagement around the campaign.

Supporters of the Labour Party from across the state attended the event. The gathering was described by those providing the event update as a successful flag-off, with a large turnout of party supporters.

National Party Leadership in Attendance

The visit brought together members of the Labour Party's National Working Committee and other party figures. Governor Alex Chioma Otti, who serves as the Governor of Abia State and the party's National Leader, was also present.

The event provided an opportunity for party leaders and supporters to come together around the 2027 National Assembly campaign of Comrade Collins Chimaobi Opara, the Labour Party candidate for AMAC/Bwari Federal Constituency in the Federal Capital Territory.

Campaign Flag-off in Owerri

The photographs from the event capture speeches, interactions among party representatives, ceremonial activities and group moments involving Labour Party officials and supporters.

The campaign gathering in Owerri forms part of the activities surrounding Comrade Collins Chimaobi Opara's participation in the 2027 National Assembly election process.
