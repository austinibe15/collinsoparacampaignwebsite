NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about volunteering and participating in campaign activities.

Who can register as a volunteer?

Anyone interested in supporting campaign activities and contributing positively to community engagement can submit the volunteer registration form. Any applicable eligibility requirements will be communicated by the campaign team.

Do I need previous campaign experience?

Previous campaign experience is not necessarily required. You can indicate your skills, interests, and experience in the registration form so the team can identify suitable opportunities.

Can I volunteer in my ward or community?

Yes. You can provide your ward and preferred volunteer area in the registration form. The campaign team can use this information when coordinating relevant local activities.

How will the campaign team contact me?

The team may use the phone number or email address you provide to contact you about volunteer opportunities and campaign activities, subject to your consent.

How can I support the campaign financially?

Please use only the official campaign contribution channel when it has been verified and published. Do not send money to unverified personal accounts.
